The five dimensions

Each scores 0 to 1. The trust score is their weighted average.

1

Completeness

Are patient ID, name, date of birth and sex present?

2

Temporal validity

Do birth, admission and discharge dates make sense and come in a sensible order?

3

Identity consistency

Do the identifier format and demographics agree, for example an Emirates ID year against the date of birth?

4

Provenance

How much do you trust the facility the record came from?

5

Cross-record consistency

Does the same identifier appear elsewhere with conflicting details? If so, it is quarantined.

What this page is, and is not

A browser demonstration of the framework from the preprint Decoupling Record Trust from Record Linkage. It is a re-implementation for demonstration, with placeholder thresholds and weights. The validated results (collision rejection of 96.0% and 92.6% against 53.4% and 30.7% for Fellegi-Sunter) come from the Python engine in the repository, on synthetic data. Nothing here has been tested on real patient data, so please use synthetic or de-identified files.
